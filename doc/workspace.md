# Workspace

## Opening repositories

On first launch, Diffinch shows an empty start screen where you can open an existing checkout, initialize a folder, or clone a repository. The **+** button beside the repository tabs also offers these actions and recent repositories. Each checkout opens in its own tab, and opening the same checkout again switches to its existing tab. When Diffinch restarts, it reopens your repository tabs in order and selects the last active tab. Closing the last tab returns to the start screen.

Drag a repository tab left or right to change its position. The insertion marker shows where it will land; hold near either edge to scroll through more tabs. Press Escape or release outside the tab row to cancel. You can also focus a tab and press **Alt+Shift+Left/Right** (Option+Shift+Left/Right on macOS). Reordering keeps the current repository selected and saves the new order for the next launch.

## Preferences and navigation

Choose Blue (the default), White, or Dark in the title bar. Diffinch remembers your theme across restarts.

Use the language picker in the title bar to switch between **English**, **한국어**, and **日本語**. Diffinch starts in your preferred system language when it is supported, defaults to English otherwise, and remembers your selection.

Commit dates and times use `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss` in every language, showing local time with a space between the date and time and no timezone suffix (for example, `2026-11-13 11:34:00`).

Click **Manual** in the title bar for an in-app guide to opening a repository, reviewing and staging changes, making commits, browsing history, and syncing with a remote.

Drag the right edge of the left panel to resize it across all views. Its width is remembered across restarts. Double-click the divider to reset, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for larger steps), Home/End, and Enter to reset.

The left panel’s **Repository** section starts collapsed. Click its heading to expand or collapse it; the choice is remembered per repository. In narrow windows its navigation icons stay available. Open repositories from the **+** menu beside the tabs or with **⌘/Ctrl+O**.
